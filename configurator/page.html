Build your own

Pick an Omniwall part, set its size and insert, and build it here in your browser. It runs the same build123d scripts as every model in this catalog, so you get the same checks and a Bambu Studio 3MF with the H2C settings, plus STLs and a STEP.

What hangs on the wall
Size and insert
Starting the CAD engine

Nothing built yet. Choose the options on the left and press Build. The first build waits for the CAD engine to load, about 30 seconds; after that, a part takes 10 to 40 seconds.
