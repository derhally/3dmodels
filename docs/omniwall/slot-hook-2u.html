Slot hook 2U

Slot hook 1U carried down into the next slot in line: one hook held in two slots, steadier against tipping out.

print-tested

The hook: dovetail front on the right, finger and foot on the left go through the panel

How it works

The top fills the upper slot like Slot hook 1U, with no snap of its own. One pitch lower, a firmer foot snaps over the lower slot's bottom rim (snap E: 4.0 mm foot, 0.10 mm grip on the steel, 0.9 mm lip with a 0.4 mm flat tip). One dovetail front runs the whole 45.7 mm, with notch pairs where two Slot hook 1Us' would be.

Was called: slot hook 2, two-slot hook.

Specs

Size2.95 × 11.15 × 45.8 mm
Volume988.5 mm³
Slots used2, in one column
Snap rows2
Fits2-row housings with the seat at the slot's top (one per column)

Printing

On its flat side, no supports. Both sides chamfered 0.5 mm. Arachne walls, 5+ walls or 100% infill; PETG if the snap cracks.
