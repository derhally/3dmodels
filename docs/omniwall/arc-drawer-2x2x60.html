Arc drawer 2×2×60

Two cells wide, two rows tall, 60 mm deep, on a single hook column.

design

Closed, on the panel

How it works

Works like the 3×2: hangs on Bridge hook 1Us in the one slot column inside it (on its centre line), slides out, tilts 30° and stays captive. Its scoop is 40 mm wide so it fits the narrower face.

Specs

Housing50.5 × 60 × 50.5 mm (W × D × H)
Drawer inside39.5 × 41.4 × 40 mm
Travel31.9 mm, then a 30° tilt
HooksBridge hook 1U, 1 column, 1 or 2 rows
Grid2 × 2 cells

Printing

Housing on its back, drawer upright, scoop on its back. No supports.
