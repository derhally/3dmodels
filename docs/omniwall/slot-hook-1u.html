Slot hook 1U

Fills a single slot and snaps over its bottom rim, so an object can sit within one cell.

print-tested

The hook: dovetail front on the right, finger and foot on the left go through the panel

How it works

A finger goes up behind the panel above the slot, and the body fills the slot's full 15.5 mm height. A cavity open to the back lets the foot flex: push the bottom in, the 25° ramp rides over the slot's bottom rim, and the foot drops with the rim in its notch.

Was called: slot hook 1, one-slot hook.
