Omniwall parts

Printed parts for the Omniwall steel pegboard (25.4 mm grid of 3.0 × 15.5 mm slots and 6.2 mm peg holes, 18 ga steel). Hooks go into the panel's slots; everything else slides down onto the hooks through dovetail pockets in its back and clicks into place.

Hooks

Every hook carries the same dovetail front, so any object's pocket fits any hook. Two families, named for how they hold the panel, and a size in U: the number of housing rows the hook's front spans, with one snap notch pair per row.

Drawers

Arc drawers: a housing that hangs on the hooks and a drawer that slides out on two rear pins and tilts down 30° at the end of its travel. Units tile edge to edge with 0.3 mm between them.
