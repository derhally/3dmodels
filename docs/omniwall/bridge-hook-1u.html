Bridge hook 1U

Clamps the steel bridge between two slots in line. The hook today's arc drawers hang on.

print-tested

The hook: dovetail front on the right, finger and foot on the left go through the panel

How it works

The top bar goes through the upper slot and rests on its bottom edge, with a finger hanging behind the panel. The finger leaves a 1.5 mm gap for the 1.55 mm steel, so the hook clamps the panel and doesn't wobble. The foot goes through the slot below and sits under that slot's top edge, so a bump from underneath can't lift the mount off. Put it on by putting the finger through the upper slot and swinging the foot into the lower one.

Was called: bridge hook, cross-slot hook.
