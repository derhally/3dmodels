Arc drawer 3×2

Three cells wide, two rows tall, 84 mm deep. The deepest drawer.

design

Closed, on the panel

How it works

The housing hangs on Bridge hook 1Us in two slot columns, in the top row or both rows. Pull the scoop and the drawer slides out on two rear pins running in grooves in the housing walls; at the end of its travel it tilts down 30° and stays captive. To take it out, tilt it fully, push it back along the gate, lift it round the stop and pull it forward.

Specs

Housing75.9 × 84 × 50.5 mm (W × D × H)

Travel55.9 mm, then a 30° tilt
HooksBridge hook 1U, 2 columns, 1 or 2 rows
Grid3 × 2 cells, 0.3 mm gap to neighbours

Printing

Housing on its back, drawer upright, scoop on its back, snap pins flat. No supports. One plate with every part in the all-parts 3MF.
