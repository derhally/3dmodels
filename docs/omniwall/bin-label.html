Front label

A white plate with black text inlaid flush, for the bins' label pocket and the U holder on the arc drawers' face.

ready to print

m3-screws-arc-drawer

How it works

Add the text to labels/bin-label/params.yaml and run its build.py. The text is Arial Bold, as large as fits the plate's margins (up to 6.5 mm tall), cut 0.4 mm into the face. A drawer label drops into its holder from above, face out.

Specs

Plate34.7 × 10.85 × 0.9 mm
Text inlay0.4 mm (two layers)
Text margins1.2 mm (bin), 2.2 mm at the sides (arc drawer)
Coloursplate filament 2 (white), text filament 1 (black)

Printing

Face down, on a smooth plate for a smooth face. The colour change is only in the first two layers. No supports.
