Bridge hook 2U

Bridge hook 1U with its front raised 12 mm to the top of the upper slot, so it fits 2-row housings made for slot hooks.

test print

The hook: dovetail front on the right, finger and foot on the left go through the panel

How it works

Goes on like Bridge hook 1U. The part behind the panel is the same; the dovetail front rises to the upper slot's top, with a second notch pair one cell lower. The front has to run down to the foot (it's the only link to it), so only one fits per column of a housing: a second one, or a Slot hook 1U, one row below would overlap it by 4.3 mm.

Was called: tall bridge hook.

On its flat side, no supports, like Bridge hook 1U.
