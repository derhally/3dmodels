Omniwall panel

The steel panel: a 25.4 mm grid, each cell boxed in by four 3.0 × 15.5 mm slots with a 6.2 mm peg hole in its middle.

reference

Omniwall panel

How it works

Neighbouring cells share slots. The hole sits midway between the slots on both axes, so slot centrelines lie on the grid lines and the holes on a grid offset by half a pitch. Every part takes its numbers from panel.yaml rather than retyping them.
